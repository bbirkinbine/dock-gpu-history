# Privacy Policy — GPU Dock History

_Last updated: 2026-07-17_

GPU Dock History is a macOS menu-free utility that displays live GPU
utilization as a graph in the Dock tile.

## Data collection

**GPU Dock History collects no data.** The app does not gather, store,
transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
- No account, sign-in, or user identifiers.
- No network connections of any kind. The app makes no outbound requests.

## How the app works

The app reads the current GPU utilization percentage from the local system
using Apple's public IOKit registry APIs and renders it as a history graph
on its own Dock icon. This reading happens entirely on your Mac and never
leaves the device. Nothing is written to disk beyond ordinary macOS
application state.

## Third parties

The app bundles no third-party SDKs and contacts no third-party services.
